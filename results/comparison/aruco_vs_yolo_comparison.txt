TARGET LOCALIZATION / REPEATABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Published summary values are not directly rankable because the nominal-center definitions differ.
The official ArUco analyzer measures marker-center distance from stored benchmark_nominal_center_px values. The official YOLO analyzer derives a separate empirical nominal center for each location from the four official trials. These published repeatability values therefore do not share the same nominal reference definition.
Published ArUco marker-center distance: mean=2.362 px, max=5.805 px.
Published YOLO bbox-center distance: mean=1.485 px, max=5.026 px.
Published YOLO upper-quarter grasp-point distance: mean=1.503 px, max=5.057 px.

Harmonized descriptive comparison:
All three coordinate types use the arithmetic mean of the four official trials at each location as their empirical nominal.
- ArUco marker center: overall mean distance=1.269 px, overall max=3.973 px.
- YOLO bbox center: overall mean distance=1.485 px, overall max=5.026 px.
- YOLO upper-quarter grasp point: overall mean distance=1.503 px, overall max=5.057 px.
